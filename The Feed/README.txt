FROM MY EYES — Allan Fung / purejoy510
Project 1 / Step 5

Open index.html, then press START to view the feed.
Keep the folders together. No installation or build is needed.
For a local server, run: python3 start-preview.py

CONTROLS
PRESS START or Enter opens the feed.
INSERT COIN or C adds a credit and shows the coin sketch.
Category buttons filter the memories. FAVOURITES shows saved moments.
SAVE THIS MOMENT keeps a favourite in this browser.
RANDOM opens one of the visible memories.
Click a memory to open it. Use Next/Previous or the arrow keys to browse.
Escape or Close returns to the feed.
Feed interactions pause background motion for one second.
PAUSE ANIMATION switches the GIFs to still images.
The site also follows the browser's reduced-motion preference.

MUSIC
SOUND controls Spotify; PLAYER opens the embedded player and track link.
Spotify needs internet access and may require a click before playing.
Music restarts when moving between pages.
